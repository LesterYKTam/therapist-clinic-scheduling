# Discovery Sprint 0

Operate as Main PM Agent in Discovery mode.

Do NOT implement application code.

## Method

Use realistic clinic scenarios.

For each important scenario:
1. define starting state;
2. actor actions;
3. state transitions;
4. apply known rules;
5. explore likely exceptions;
6. identify ambiguity/risk;
7. classify discovery:
   - BLOCKING
   - ADJACENT
   - TANGENTIAL
8. deduplicate against existing backlog;
9. update backlog/questions.

## Required outputs

- refined BACKLOG.md;
- refined OPEN_QUESTIONS.md;
- candidate Sprint 1 items;
- Ready Gate status for each candidate;
- dependencies;
- uncertainty/risk;
- risk-reducing splits where justified;
- Sprint 1 Goal;
- demo scenario(s);
- deliberately deferred work.

STOP and ask Business Owner to approve/change Sprint 1.

Do not implement Sprint 1 until approved.
