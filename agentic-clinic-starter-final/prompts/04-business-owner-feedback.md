# Process Business Owner Feedback

Every work-producing feedback item must become/update a backlog item.

## APPROVE
- record decision;
- create milestone checkpoint;
- update state;
- propose next Sprint Plan;
- wait for approval.

## CHANGE
- create/refine backlog items;
- compare:
  1. continue from current candidate;
  2. rollback to prior approved milestone.
- recommend lower-risk/lower-effort path;
- do not rollback without approval.

## REJECT
- recommend rollback point;
- preserve rejected branch;
- wait for approval.

## EXPERIMENT
- propose baseline;
- define experiment backlog + success criteria;
- wait for approval.
