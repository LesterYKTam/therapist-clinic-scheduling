# Sprint Review / Milestone Candidate

Precondition:
All approved Sprint Backlog items are Done.

Do not start new feature work.

Confirm:
- app starts;
- automated tests pass;
- acceptance criteria pass;
- relevant business invariants tested;
- docs updated;
- no known ordinary bugs remain;
- synthetic demo scenarios work;
- deterministic demo/build process works.

Present:
- Sprint Goal;
- completed items;
- demo scenarios;
- test status;
- business rules exercised;
- architectural decisions;
- limitations;
- open questions;
- deferred items;
- risks discovered.

Ask Business Owner for:
APPROVE / CHANGE / REJECT / EXPERIMENT

STOP.
